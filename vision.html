Can a vision model read the boundary from pictures?

Ata's tool fed Google Earth's 3D view from above and from four angles, plus Street View for the front boundary, to a vision LLM, which classified each boundary a flow path crosses. Heights come from the laser survey. Here are its calls on Mt Albert properties, next to what our laser and photo model (v12) found on the same boundaries.

How to read this

Vision model's call is its own label and its own confidence, which it states is subjective and uncalibrated. Where it gave a reason, the bubble shows it.

Ours is v12 along the same boundary, within 2 m: metres of fence or wall, hedge, can't tell and open. "Agrees" means our main call for what stands there matches the vision model's.

Mixed is the vision model saying more than one thing stands along the boundary, which v12 shows as several stretches.

A handful of properties only. It shows how the two methods line up, not how accurate either is. Not authoritative.
