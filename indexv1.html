What this assumes

Each building is a solid block 3 m tall, from LINZ outlines burnt onto the 1 m grid. Water goes round it, and rain on the roof runs off the downhill edge. Real houses leak: water gets under floors on piles and in through doors.

A building counts as on a flow path when a ground-only path draining 2,000 m² or more, Council's cut-off, crosses its outline.

Still no fences, kerbs or stormwater pipes: water moves to the lowest neighbouring square metre, as if every drain were already full.

Hollows assume culverts are blocked, so a road embankment across a gully shows as a dam. Catchment sizes only count land inside this map.
